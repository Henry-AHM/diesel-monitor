Method and sources

Diesel: MBIE weekly main-port board price (c/L, incl. GST), or the discount-adjusted retail price, from MBIE weekly fuel price monitoring. Monthly bucket = mean of weekly (Friday) observations. Weeks from Jul 2026 are marked provisional by MBIE.

Brent: daily Europe Brent spot, FRED DCOILBRENTEU. FX: daily US$ per NZ$, FRED DEXUSNZ. NZ c/L equivalent = Brent ÷ USD/NZD ÷ 158.987 L × 100.

Lags: weekly diesel is interpolated to daily, then averaged over the calendar month shifted forward 30 or 60 days. Percentage changes are month-on-month. Transmission ratio = diesel % change ÷ Brent % change (in NZD). A ratio below 1 is expected, because GST, margins and freight dilute the crude share of the pump price. Ratios are hidden where Brent moved less than 2%. Lagged values use MBIE data to ; a lag that runs past the latest week is left blank.

: averages use the latest published observations only: MBIE diesel to , Brent spot to , USD/NZD to . Figures will change as later weeks are released. Brent futures were not spliced in, because front-month futures trade well below dated spot in the current market.

Shaded band, Mar-Apr 2026: Brent rose 63% in March, its largest monthly gain since 1988, as the Iran war disrupted Gulf supply (CNBC; EIA). Stats NZ reported diesel prices up 36.6% from March to April 2026 (Stats NZ).
